BusyPico

A toy with lights and switches

I am look to build a handheld, battery-powered game console based on the Raspberry Pi Pico 2. This will allow me to develop my understanding of electronics and programming.

The steps taken will be

  1. Components
    The components are selected to ensure they are suitable and work together
  2. Prototype
    A prototype is designed and created to test the circuit is correct
  3. Chassis
    A chassis is designed and printed
  4. Wiring
    The electronics are soldered to a permanent perfboard
  5. Software
    Codes is written to talk to the hardware to ensure it functions as intended
  6. Game
    Games are written and tested on the device
  7. Assembly
    Games are uploaded, batteries inserted and the chassis closed!
