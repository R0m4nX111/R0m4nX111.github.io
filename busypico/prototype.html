Prototype

Batteries

The 2x AAA batteries are connected in series giving 3.0 V (2x 1.5 V) and a capacity of 1000 mAh.

The Pico accepts 1.8 V to 5.5 V via the VSYS pin. The 74HC595 operates between 2.0 V and 6.0 V. The LEDs (red, orange and yellow) operate between 1.8 V and 2.2 V. All are within the capacity of the chosen batteries.

Shift Register

The 74HC595 is an 8-bit shift register (SR) driven by an SPI TX connection. The SPI (Serial Periphal Interface) works by pulsing (or not pulsing) the data pin synchronously with the clock pin to commuicate the desired byte into the register bit by bit. Once transfered the shift register assigns the HIGH or LOW voltage to the output pins.

The serial out (Q7') is not required as it is used to "daisy chain" shift registers.

Lights

8x LEDs are arranged in a 2x4 grid. The rows will be red, orange, yellow, green. Given the 3.3 V input voltage from the Pico the resistors will be per the table below.
Colour Forward Voltage Target Current Resistor Value
Red 1.8 V 8 mA 180 Ω
Orange 2.0 V 7 mA 180 Ω
Yellow 2.1 V 8 mA 150 Ω
Green 2.2 V 10 mA 100 Ω
